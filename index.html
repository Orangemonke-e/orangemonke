v23 New15 Nov 2026

Added

  • 💾 Presets: save your design with a name and load it again later. You can keep up to 30, each with a little preview picture, and delete any you don't want.
  • Auto-save: close the page and come back, and your last design is still there.
  • Presets save your colours, background style, accessories, dog, halo, outline and name tag. Uploaded images aren't saved.

Changed

  • The name tag now uses a blocky pixel font (Press Start 2P), closer to the names in Gorilla Tag. Long names still shrink to fit.

Added

Fixed

  • The name tag now sits at the top-left of the torso, like the name on a gorilla's chest. Before, it was at the bottom centre. Long names shrink to fit.

  • Random Monke now gives the halo a random colour and glow.
